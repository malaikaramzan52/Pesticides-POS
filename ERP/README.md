# 🌱 Agro Pesticides POS

A modern **Point of Sale (POS) & Inventory Management System** designed for **Pesticides, Fertilizers, Seeds, Agro Chemicals, and Agricultural Product Dealers**. The system streamlines sales, purchases, inventory, customer management, supplier management, expenses, reporting, and financial tracking through an intuitive and responsive interface.
